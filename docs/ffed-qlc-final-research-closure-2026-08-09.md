## Identity decision

Vigil is the only mascot and visible assistant identity owned by FfeD-QLC. The
former five-guardian interpretation is rejected for this product direction.
Detection, policy, continuity and routing are internal capabilities behind
Vigil, not separate personalities.

Vigil may request a bounded handoff from an existing mascot owned by another
Education tool. The source repository must verify the mascot name, tool,
contract and allowed metrics. Current examples verified in the suite include:

- Qbit in FNP-QNN-MVP and the AlgoQuest integration surface;
- RaySight in Visual Algorithm Designer;
- Orion Vey in QuaNThoR.

The remote mascot gathers only consented information inside its own tool for a
student or professor. It returns selected competency IDs, evidence references,
a bounded metric summary, a limitation and a safe next action. Full account
history, private conversations, raw rosters, hidden grades and secrets are
forbidden. No history triggers a transparent local baseline, not a failure.

## Research decisions

1. FQLC1 remains a working educational object. ChaCha20-Poly1305 supplies
   confidentiality and integrity; scrypt derives the passphrase key; the
   structural permutation has no independently established security margin.
2. The public orb remains a redacted audit and visualization surface.
3. A future multi-recipient design must use a random content key, authenticated
   content encryption and maintained standard recipient wrapping.
4. Recipient removal is not revocation of information already learned. Future
   confidentiality can require a new content key and re-encryption.
5. Exact integral Apollonian traces are the preferred research candidate for a
   public, versioned geometry context. Geometry is not secret entropy.
6. Deterministic CBOR (RFC 8949) and JCS (RFC 8785) are both viable serialization
   references. The protocol must select one, ban ambiguous numeric forms and
   publish cross-language golden vectors before interoperability claims.
7. Vigil uses one provider-neutral Wakeup Kit for Codex and Gemini. Model
   disagreement creates a professor-review record; it is never averaged away.
8. The curriculum measures tasks, knowledge, skills, attacks, evidence and
   limitations. The professor owns grades, publication and exceptions.
9. Portfolio and Azure progression remain learner-selected and secret-free.
   Azure deployment should prefer workload identity/OIDC over long-lived keys.

## Gates intentionally left for measurement

- scrypt profiles require wall-time and memory benchmarks on target hardware;
- the Apollonian trace requires two exact encoders and classroom evaluation;
- recipient implementation requires a dependency/interoperability TDR;
- portfolio retention requires an institution-approved policy;
- workload and accommodations require a learner/professor pilot;
- Azure region, retention and cost require an approved deployment profile;
- AI educational benefit requires controlled comparison and human review.

These are implementation or pilot gates, not blockers for the research article.
